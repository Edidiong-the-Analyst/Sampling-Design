Sampling Design Summary
========================

Design: two-arm (treatment vs. control), cluster-level, single post-baseline comparison feeding into a DiD analysis (see script 03).
Baseline mCPR assumed:      25%
Minimum detectable effect:  8 percentage points
Alpha / Power:               0.05 / 0.80
Assumed ICC:                 0.020
Design effect:               4.58
Clusters required per arm:   13
Total clusters:              26
Total planned respondents:   4680
